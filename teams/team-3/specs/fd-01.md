## Content that loads late

## Renders in place

- **View All** does not navigate and does not change the list: URL stays `/`, still 5 cards
  (the landing already shows every restaurant). A test can only assert all 5 restaurants are
  listed after clicking it.

## Where the app already contradicts the spec

- Spec: "A restaurant that does not deliver to the current address is shown greyed out … It cannot be
  opened." — The *Koliba u Jána* card is a working link; selecting it opens
  `/restaurant/koliba-u-jana` with a full, orderable menu (quick-add puts a dish in the cart).
- Spec: "A card shows the restaurant's current promotion when it has one". Koliba u Jána's page
  shows the promotion *20% off your first order*, its card does not (the badge takes its place).
  Ambiguous whether an unavailable restaurant's card must still show it — checkpoint candidate.
- "Greyed out" is only a grayscale filter on the card image; the text is at full colour. Soft
  finding; a test can check the image's computed `filter`.
