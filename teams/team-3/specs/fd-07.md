## Storage and the total

Nothing is stored: localStorage and sessionStorage are empty after placing an order. The total
paid comes **only** from the `total` query parameter:

| Address | Total Paid |
| --- | --- |
| `/order/FDR-FTPU3T?total=15.48` (via Track My Order) | $15.48 |
| `/order/FDR-FTPU3T?total=0.01` | **$0.01** |
| `/order/FDR-FTPU3T` (no query) | **$0.00** |

## Content that loads late

Tracking details render after a moment; wait for `Total Paid`.

## Where the app already contradicts the spec

- "**Total paid** is the amount of the placed order. It cannot be changed by editing the address in
  the browser." — editing `?total=` changes Total Paid to anything; dropping it shows $0.00.
- "Tracking works only for real orders. An order number that was never placed does not show a
  tracking page." — `/order/FDR-000000` and even `/order/not-an-order` show a full tracking page
  (`Order #not-an-order`, Total Paid $0.00, all five stages).
- The estimated delivery is `25-35 min` for every restaurant (Mediterranean Delight advertises
  35-45, Sushi Masters 20-30); the spec only says "the estimated delivery time", so this is a
  reading, not a hard contradiction.
