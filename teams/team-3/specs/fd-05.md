# FD-05 · Cart — exploration notes

Explored against https://foodora.lovable.app on 2026-09-24, with Playwright MCP from a clean state
(localStorage and sessionStorage cleared).

## Routes

The cart has no route: it opens as a panel (`dialog`) over the current page from the header
button. Proceed to Checkout navigates to `/checkout`.

Restaurants used (restaurant page `Delivery Fee` value in brackets):
`/restaurant/1` Burger Palace ($2.99, *20% OFF orders over $25*), `/restaurant/2` Pizza Corner
(Free), `/restaurant/3` Sushi Masters ($1.99), `/restaurant/4` Mediterranean Delight ($3.49).
Dishes: Burger Palace `bp-1` Classic Beef Burger $12.95, `bp-3` Veggie Supreme $11.99, `bp-4`
Double Smash Burger $15.99; Pizza Corner `pc-1` Margherita $13.99; Sushi Masters `sm-1`
California Roll $10.99; Mediterranean Delight first dish Chicken Shawarma Plate $15.99.
Quick-add is an unnamed `button` inside the dish `link` — locate it as
`a[href="/product/bp-1"] button` (or `getByRole('link', { name: /Classic Beef Burger/ }).getByRole('button')`).

## Elements (role and accessible name as the snapshot shows them)

Header:

- `button "Cart"` when empty; `button "Cart 1"` / `"Cart 2"` (text `Cart` + a badge) when not.
  The badge counts **units** (Classic Beef ×2 → `Cart 2`).

Cart panel:

- `dialog` (no accessible name), `heading "Your Cart (N)" [level=2]` — N counts **lines**, not
  units (Classic Beef ×3 → `Your Cart (1)` while the header says 3).
- Each line: `img "<dish>"`, `heading "<dish>" [level=4]`, `paragraph "<restaurant>"`, the
  **unit** price (`$12.95` stays `$12.95` at qty 3), then three **unnamed** buttons: minus
  (`svg.lucide-minus`), the quantity as text, plus (`svg.lucide-plus`), remove
  (`svg.lucide-trash2`). No `aria-label` on any of them.
- − at quantity 1 removes the line. The trash button removes the line.
- `button "Clear Cart"` — only with 2+ different dishes; not shown with one dish even at qty 2.
  Clicking it empties the cart (header back to `Cart`).
- Summary rows (text, not labelled): `Subtotal`, `Delivery Fee`, `Service Fee`, `Total`;
  `button "Proceed to Checkout"`; `button "Close"`. Escape closes the panel.
- Empty: `heading "Your Cart (0)"`, `heading "Your cart is empty" [level=3]`, *Add some delicious
  items to get started!*, `button "Continue Shopping"` (closes the panel, stays on the current
  page — on `/` that is the restaurant list). No Proceed to Checkout, no summary.

## Content that loads late

- Restaurant pages render the header first; wait for a dish name before quick-adding.
- After a quick-add a toast *Added to cart!* / *<dish> has been added to your cart.* appears in
  `region "Notifications (F8)"`.

## Storage

localStorage and sessionStorage stay **empty** with items in the cart; only cookies `__dpl` and
`session-id` exist. Reloading `/restaurant/1` with 3 items gives header `Cart` and an empty panel.

## Where the app already contradicts the spec

- "The **Delivery Fee** is the fee the restaurant advertises — **Free** means $0.00." — the cart
  always charges $2.99 (Pizza Corner advertises Free, Sushi Masters $1.99, Mediterranean $3.49).
- "*20% OFF orders over $25* takes 20 % off the subtotal once it passes $25, and the discount
  shows as its own line." — Burger Palace subtotals $25.90, $36.93, $40.93 get no discount and no
  discount line (expected e.g. $25.90 → −$5.18, Total $25.21).
- "The cart survives a page reload: refreshing the browser does not lose the order." — a reload
  empties the cart.
- "a quantity stepper (**−** / **+**), and a way to remove it" — the controls exist but have no
  accessible name (also an FD-03 "every button … has an accessible name" issue).
- Not a spec rule, but worth noting: the panel heading `Your Cart (N)` counts lines while the
  header button counts units.
